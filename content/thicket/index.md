# The Thicket

**The thicket is the network of blygs: every blyg, and every quote, pin, fork
and mention that ties one to another.** It used to be called the
*blygosphere*. The new name comes from [Aneesh Sathe](https://blyg.aneeshsathe.com/),
who wrote of the "healthy thicket feels" of connected blygs, and of a blyg post
as humus, "left there to decompose, combine, nourish, and be taken up
elsewhere. It does not need to know what it will become. It only needs to be
available to the thicket."

## An explorer is coming

This page will hold a tool for exploring and searching the thicket. It takes
its cue from blockchain block explorers. Those tools let anyone take a public
ledger nobody owns, follow any transaction back to where its inputs came from,
and see what each address has touched. The thicket is a public record in the
same sense. Every item has an address, a version history and a changelog.
Every quote is a snapshot of a named version, with a record of where it came
from, and forks and pins name exact versions. The structure is already in the
files. What's missing is a way to see it.

Things the explorer might show:

- **An item:** its versions and pins, every place it has been quoted or
  forked, and how those quotes spread from blyg to blyg.
- **A blyg:** what it quotes, who quotes it, its blogroll and its mentions.
- **A thread:** the fragments it is built from, traced back through quotes of
  quotes to their origins.
- **The whole thicket:** search across every listed blyg, plus the shapes the
  linking makes over time: clusters, bridges, the most-quoted items.
